Down Payment Assistance in Sioux Falls & South Dakota

You may need much less saved than you think. Here are the programs that can help you buy in Sioux Falls, explained in plain language.

Check What I Qualify For

"First-time buyer" usually means you have not owned a home in the past three years, so even past owners may qualify. Most programs have income and price limits, and rules change, so always confirm the current details with a lender before you count on them.

Down payment help programs

New

Give Me Five

South Dakota Housing's newest program, started November 2, 2026. Eligible first-time buyers can get down payment help equal to 5% of their mortgage.

  • No monthly payments
  • Forgiven after five years if you stay in the home and follow program rules
  • Homes up to $410,000; income limits apply (about $103,400 to $144,760 depending on county and household size)
  • Temporary: up to 2,500 loans statewide, so do not wait

SD Housing First-Time Homebuyer Program

Low, fixed-rate loans through participating local lenders, with optional down payment and closing cost help (commonly 3% or 5% of the loan, as a 0% interest loan).

  • Works with FHA, Conventional, VA, and USDA loans
  • Purchase price and income limits apply
  • A Mortgage Credit Certificate (MCC) may lower your federal income tax

SD Housing Repeat Homebuyer Program

Already owned a home? This program offers competitive rates, flexible down payment options, possible down payment help, and reduced mortgage insurance for qualified buyers.

GROW South Dakota

A nonprofit that offers zero-interest, deferred loans for down payment and closing costs to income-qualified buyers across South Dakota. Funds are limited and come in rounds.

City of Sioux Falls: Public Safety

For full-time certified police officers and firefighters. Up to $20,000 or 20% of the price (whichever is less) for homes in certain Sioux Falls neighborhoods, up to $385,000. Apply at least four weeks before closing.

Low and no down payment loans

VA loan

$0 down for eligible veterans, service members, and some surviving spouses. No monthly mortgage insurance. Read my VA guide →

USDA loan

$0 down for homes in eligible rural areas. Some towns around Sioux Falls may qualify; income limits apply. Check any address on the USDA eligibility map.

FHA

As little as 3.5% down with more flexible credit guidelines. Can be combined with down payment assistance.

Conventional

As little as 3% down for qualified first-time buyers. Mortgage insurance can be removed once you reach 20% equity.

How to get started

  1. Talk to a lender who works with South Dakota Housing programs. They check which programs you qualify for.
  2. Get pre-approved with the assistance built in, so you know your real price range.
  3. Take a homebuyer education class if your program requires it (Sioux Empire Housing Partnership offers one locally).
  4. Shop for homes under the program limits with me, then we write a strong offer.
