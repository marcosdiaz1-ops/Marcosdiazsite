Buyer Guide

What to know before you buy.

Buying a home is a big decision. Here's what I walk every client through before we start, and what to expect once you're under contract.

Ask Me Anything Get my free Buyer's Guide (PDF) → Down payment help programs → Buying with low credit → Buying with an ITIN →

Considerations for buyers

Job & income

Do I have a stable job and income? Am I staying in this line of work? What are my expenses, savings, and debts?

Credit score

A credit score of 620 is typically required for conventional loans, while FHA loans have more flexibility depending on down payment.

Down payment

Depending on loan type, your down payment can range from 0%–20% of the purchase price.

Closing costs

Closing costs range from 2–6% of the loan amount. Other fees include inspection and appraisal costs.

Long term planning

Do I plan to stay in this location? Do I foresee major life changes? Consider how your career, family, and lifestyle might evolve over time.

Ongoing costs

Your budget should factor in monthly mortgage payments, property taxes, homeowner's insurance, and maintenance costs (roughly 1%–2% of purchase price per year).

The closing process

1Escrow

Buyer deposits earnest money into a neutral escrow account, which holds funds until the transaction is complete.

2Inspections

Buyer orders an inspection to ensure the property's condition is clear of major issues.

3Loan approval & appraisal

The lender orders an appraisal to confirm the home's value meets or exceeds the purchase price, then approves the buyer for the mortgage.

4Final walkthrough

Before closing, the buyer conducts a final walkthrough to confirm the property's condition is as expected.

5Closing day

Buyer and seller sign documents. Buyer pays closing costs, receives keys, and takes possession, while the seller receives the sale proceeds.

6Packing & moving

Start packing up and planning the move. Congratulations!

Seller Guide

Sell with a plan, not a guess.

Selling your home is about more than putting a sign in the yard. Here's how I help you price it right, prepare it well, and get it sold on your terms.

What's My Home Worth? Estimate my net proceeds → Get my free Seller's Guide (PDF) →

Before you list

Pricing it right

Price is the biggest factor in how fast your home sells and for how much. I'll prepare a comparative market analysis using recent sales, active competition, and current buyer demand in your neighborhood, so your price is based on data, not guesswork.

Preparing your home

Small improvements can make a big difference: decluttering, deep cleaning, fresh paint, and simple repairs help buyers picture themselves in your home. I'll walk through with you and point out what's worth doing and what isn't.

Timing your sale

When you list matters. We'll look at the market, your next move, and your schedule to choose the right time, and plan how to coordinate selling with buying your next home if you're doing both.

Knowing your bottom line

Before we list, I'll help you estimate your net proceeds by walking through your mortgage payoff, closing costs, and other selling expenses, so there are no surprises at the closing table.

Disclosures

South Dakota requires sellers to complete a property condition disclosure statement. I'll guide you through it so you can be accurate and confident about what you share with buyers.

The selling process

1Consultation & pricing

We meet, talk through your goals and timeline, tour the home, and set a pricing strategy together.

2Prepare & photograph

Your home gets ready for market, then professional photos show it at its best.

3List & market

Your listing goes live on the MLS and major home search sites, backed by marketing to put it in front of the right buyers.

4Showings & open houses

Buyers tour your home. I'll keep you updated on interest and feedback after every showing.

5Offers & negotiation

I review every offer with you, including price, terms, contingencies, and timelines, and negotiate to get you the strongest deal.

6Under contract

The buyer completes inspections and the lender orders an appraisal. I'll handle any repair requests or negotiations that come up.

7Closing day

You sign the final documents, hand over the keys, and receive your sale proceeds. Congratulations!

FAQ

Quick answers.

Do you work with Spanish-speaking buyers and sellers?

Yes. I'm bilingual and can guide you through the whole process in English or Spanish, from the first call to closing day.

How much do I need for a down payment in Sioux Falls?

Less than many people think. Some loans need 3% to 3.5% down, VA loans can be $0 down for eligible veterans, and South Dakota Housing has programs that help first-time buyers with the down payment. A lender can tell you exactly what you qualify for. About Give Me Five →

Should I get pre-approved before I look at homes?

Yes. A pre-approval tells you your price range and shows sellers you're serious. In a competitive market, offers without one are often passed over.

How are buyer's agents paid?

Buyers now sign a written agreement with their agent before touring homes, and it spells out how the agent is paid. Often the seller ends up covering some or all of it, but it's negotiable. I'll walk you through your options before we start.

How long does it take to buy a home?

Finding the right home can take a few days or a few months. Once your offer is accepted, closing often takes about 30 to 45 days, depending on your loan.

How do I find out what my home is worth?

Online estimates can be way off. Request a free home value report and I'll compare your home to real recent sales nearby. Get my home value →

What areas do you serve?

All of Sioux Falls plus Tea, Harrisburg, Brandon, Garretson, Hartford, Crooks, Lennox, Canton and nearby communities in Minnehaha and Lincoln counties.

Can you help veterans and military families?

Yes. I work with military families and veterans and can help you use your VA home loan benefit, including $0-down options for eligible buyers. Read the VA loan guide →
