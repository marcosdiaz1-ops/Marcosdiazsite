Living in Southeast Sioux Falls

Southeast Sioux Falls covers the part of the city east of Minnesota Avenue and south of about 26th Street. Closer in, along I-229, you'll find established neighborhoods from the 1960s–90s with mature trees. Farther out toward Veterans Parkway, most homes are newer construction.

This side of town has three school districts. Depending on the street, a home may be in Sioux Falls, Brandon Valley or Harrisburg schools, which is something to check early.

What to know before you buy in Southeast Sioux Falls

  • Getting around: I-229 and Veterans Parkway (SD-100) make it quick to reach I-90 and the rest of the city.
  • The river: the Big Sioux River and the city bike trail run along this side of town. Ask if a home is in a flood zone before you buy.
  • County line: some southeast homes are in Lincoln County and some in Minnehaha County, and tax rates differ.
  • New construction: farther east and south, check builder warranties, HOA rules and special assessments.
