Living in Brandon

Brandon is a community in Minnehaha County just east of Sioux Falls. Buyers often choose Brandon for its hometown feel and easy access to Sioux Falls, along with nearby outdoor spaces like the Big Sioux Recreation Area.

I can help you explore homes in Brandon and compare them with nearby options so you find the right fit for your budget and lifestyle.

Why buyers like Brandon

  • Hometown feel close to Sioux Falls
  • Near the Big Sioux Recreation Area
  • Mix of established and newer neighborhoods
  • Easy access to I-90 and the east side of Sioux Falls

Schools

Brandon is served by the Brandon Valley School District. Always confirm the school district for a specific address.
