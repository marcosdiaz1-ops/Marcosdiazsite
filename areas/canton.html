Living in Canton

Canton is the county seat of Lincoln County, about 20 miles south of Sioux Falls and 10 miles east of I-29 on U.S. Highway 18. The historic Lincoln County Courthouse is downtown, along with restored older buildings on Main Street.

The town sits in the rolling hills of the Sioux Valley, with the Big Sioux River on the east side, Newton Hills State Park six miles south and Lake Alvin to the north.

What to know before you buy in Canton

  • More homes with history: Canton has many older homes near downtown, so a good inspection (foundation, wiring, sewer line) matters.
  • Outdoors: Newton Hills State Park has trails, camping and cross-country skiing; the town also has a nine-hole golf course and a community pool.
  • Commute: plan on about 25–30 minutes to central Sioux Falls, depending on where you work.
  • Prices: homes in Canton often cost less than similar homes in Sioux Falls. See today's Canton listings for current prices.
