Living in Tea

Tea is a growing community in Lincoln County, just southwest of Sioux Falls. Many buyers choose Tea for its small-town feel while staying only minutes from Sioux Falls shopping, dining, and jobs.

With plenty of newer homes and ongoing development, Tea can be a great fit for first-time buyers, growing families, and anyone looking for a little more space.

Why buyers like Tea

  • Small-town community feel close to Sioux Falls
  • Many newer homes and neighborhoods
  • Easy access to I-29
  • Room to grow, with ongoing new development

Schools

Tea is served by the Tea Area School District. Always confirm the school district for a specific address.
