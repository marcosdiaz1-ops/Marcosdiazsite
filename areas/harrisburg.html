Living in Harrisburg

Harrisburg sits just south of Sioux Falls in Lincoln County and has been one of the fastest-growing communities in the area. Buyers are drawn to its newer neighborhoods and community feel, with Sioux Falls just a short drive away.

Whether you're buying your first home or moving up, I can help you understand the Harrisburg market and find the right neighborhood.

Why buyers like Harrisburg

  • Fast-growing community with many newer homes
  • A short drive to Sioux Falls
  • Community feel with room to grow
  • Options across a range of price points

Schools

Harrisburg is served by the Harrisburg School District, which also covers parts of southern Sioux Falls. Always confirm the school district for a specific address.
