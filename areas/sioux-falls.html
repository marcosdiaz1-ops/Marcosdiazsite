Living in Sioux Falls

Sioux Falls is South Dakota's largest city and the economic hub of the region. From historic homes near downtown and Falls Park to newer subdivisions on the edges of town, there's a wide range of neighborhoods, price points, and home styles to choose from.

Because the city is growing in every direction, the right neighborhood depends on what matters most to you: commute, schools, lot size, budget, or being close to shopping, parks, and trails.

Why buyers like Sioux Falls

  • Neighborhoods ranging from established, tree-lined streets to brand-new construction
  • Access to I-29 and I-229 for getting around the city
  • Parks, trails, and Falls Park in the heart of downtown
  • A broad mix of price points for first-time buyers, move-up buyers, and investors

Schools

Most of Sioux Falls is served by the Sioux Falls School District, but some edges of the city fall in neighboring districts such as Harrisburg, Tea Area, and Brandon Valley. Always confirm the school district for a specific address.
