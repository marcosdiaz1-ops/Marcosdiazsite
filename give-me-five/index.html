Free Give Me Five Buying Plan

How much could Give Me Five give you?

South Dakota's new Give Me Five program, started Nov. 2, 2026, can give eligible first-time buyers down payment help equal to 5% of their mortgage. Answer six quick questions and see your numbers: your estimated help, the cash you'd still need, and your monthly payment.

  • No monthly payments on the down payment help
  • Forgiven after 5 years if you stay in the home and meet program rules
  • Homes up to $410,000. Income limits apply
  • Temporary program, so funds may run out

  • Give Me Five is for buyers who haven't owned a home in the last 3 years. SD Housing's Repeat Homebuyer Program may be a better fit.
  • Veterans who owned a home recently may still qualify through SD Housing's Veterans Waiver. A participating lender can confirm.
  • The home must be your main residence. Investment and second homes don't qualify.
  • The program limit is a $410,000 purchase price. We can look at homes at or under that price.
