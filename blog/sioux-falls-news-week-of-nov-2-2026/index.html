This Week in Sioux Falls: Roads Reopen, Building Permits and New Spots (Nov. 2, 2026)

By Marcos Diaz, REALTOR® · November 6, 2026 · Ver en español

Every week I round up the Sioux Falls news that matters if you own a home here, or you're thinking about buying or selling one. Here's what happened the week of November 2, 2026, and what it means for you.

1. Road construction is wrapping up for the season

The City of Sioux Falls and the South Dakota DOT announced that most of this year's street projects are finished or close to it. A few that matter if you live or shop on the south and east sides:

  • Arrowhead Parkway and Veterans Parkway: the intersection is expected to reopen to traffic by November 20, weather permitting. Landscaping and cleanup will continue into next year.
  • I-229 and Cliff Avenue: all ramps are expected to open by November 20.
  • South Veterans Parkway (Cliff to Sycamore): the new six-lane stretch is opening now.
  • Russell Street and the 49th Street bridge are done.
  • Downtown Minnesota Avenue (2nd to 8th Streets): lanes reopen later this fall, with final cleanup in spring 2027.

What it means for homeowners: Veterans Parkway is turning into the main loop around the south and east sides of the city. Better roads make neighborhoods along it easier to get to, which buyers notice. If you're house hunting in east or southeast Sioux Falls, your commute is about to get easier.

Source: City of Sioux Falls construction update

2. More homes are getting built this year, but mostly apartments

The city reported about $98 million in building permits for October, a little below a typical October. A few numbers stand out for the housing market through the first 10 months of 2026:

  • 359 new single-family homes have been permitted, up from 323 at this point last year.
  • 1,663 apartment and multifamily units have been permitted, way up from 930 last year.
  • The biggest projects in October were apartments, including the next phase of Harvest Ridge on S. Ollerich Ave. and The Galerie on S. Loop Place.

What it means for you: A wave of new apartments gives renters more choices. Single-family construction is up only a little, though, and homes for sale are still scarce. Sioux Falls ended September with about a 3.8-month supply of homes. For sellers, that means less competition. For buyers, it means good homes still move fast, so get pre-approved before you start touring.

Source: SiouxFalls.Business

3. The Outdoor Campus finishes its expansion

South Dakota Game, Fish and Parks celebrated the end of a two-phase expansion at the Outdoor Campus in Sioux Falls. The new space includes an indoor archery range, a game processing area and three new classrooms, adding to the outdoor archery range that opened in 2025.

Why it matters: Free and low-cost things to do close to home are a big reason families pick Sioux Falls. It’s a nice perk for families anywhere in town.

Source: KELOLAND News

4. New spots on the way

Tropical Smoothie Cafe is set to open its first Sioux Falls location near East 57th Street in late November. New businesses opening in an area are usually a good sign of growth there.

Source: SiouxFalls.Business

The market in one sentence

The typical Sioux Falls home sold for about $344,600 in September, up 1.8% from a year ago, and homes are selling a bit faster than last year. See the full numbers on my Sioux Falls market page.
