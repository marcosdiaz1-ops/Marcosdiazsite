Privacy Notice

How information you share on 605homesearch.com is used. Effective November 4, 2026.

What I collect

When you fill out the contact form, I receive the answers you choose (for example, whether you're buying or selling and your timeline) along with your name, phone number, and email address. I don't collect anything else through this website.

How I use it

I use your information only to respond to you and help with your real estate needs, such as calling, texting, or emailing you about your search or sale. Your information is never sold.

Who processes it

  • Form delivery: form submissions are delivered to my email by Web3Forms, a form-processing service.
  • Hosting: this website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) for security purposes.
  • My brokerage: as a licensee of Keller Williams Realty Sioux Falls, I may keep your contact details in my brokerage's client management tools so I can serve you.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics tracking. Fonts are hosted on this site, not loaded from third parties.

Other websites

Links such as "Search Homes" (my Keller Williams home search) and Google reviews take you to other websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Your choices
